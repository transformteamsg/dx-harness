3 — which black

All four carry lime at 13:1 or better, so this is not a contrast decision either. Pure #000 leaves nowhere to go down — insets, wells and the scrim under a dialog all have to be faked with a border — and at 13.9:1 the lime edges bloom on OLED at display sizes. #060707 reads as black, keeps a four-step ladder above it, and still allows a darker inset if one is ever needed.

Notes — the decisions behind the numbers

  1. Lime is a text colour on dark and a fill colour on light. It never swaps. At 13.4:1 on #060707 the lime carries body text, links and code keywords in the dark world; on paper the same value drops to 1.4:1 and carries nothing, so the light world hands text to --lime-text #4C6B1F (5.9:1) and keeps the full-chroma lime for fills only. This is the same move aihero.dev makes with its gold: on light its primary is near-black ink, and the gold survives only as a fill.
  2. Black-on-lime, never white-on-lime. White on #A3E635 is 1.5:1 — unusable. Every lime fill takes --lime-ink, and the hover and pressed steps were picked so the ink still clears AAA on them (9.5:1 at the darkest).
  3. The worst pair is the light-mode CTA edge, and it needed a fix. A lime button on paper is 1.44:1 against its own background — the label passes at 11.8:1 but the button's shape fails the 3:1 UI-boundary floor, so on a light page the CTA dissolves into the paper. Darkening the fill until it passes stops it reading as lime. The fix is a 1px --lime-dim hairline on the light-mode fill only (5.9:1 against paper): the lime stays lime, and the boundary is carried by the edge instead of the fill. --ring has the same problem and the same answer — the light focus ring is the dark step, not the brand lime.
  4. Near-black, not pure black. #060707 over #000: it reads identically as "black", keeps a four-step ladder above it for surface, muted and hover fills, and leaves a darker step available for wells and scrims. Linear reserves #010102 for marketing pages only and runs its product on #08090a; this sits between.
  5. Warm paper, not white. #FAFAF7 and #F1F2EA carry a few points of yellow so the lime fill lands on a ground that shares its temperature. On pure white the same fill reads as a sticker.
  6. Elevation is surface steps and hairlines. No glows, no lime bloom behind the hero — the dark world separates by the 1.05/1.08 surface ladder and the --border hairline, exactly as the current .landing-dark block does.
  7. Unresolved: --success. Grass-9 #46A758 and the brand lime are the same family. A green "pass" badge next to a lime brand mark stops meaning "pass". Either the success semantic moves onto the lime itself, or it moves away to teal. Not settled here.
