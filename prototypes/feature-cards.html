Open questions for review

  • Card 2's tier strip repeats words already in the full-map diagram lower on the page (L0/L1/L2, "it depends" / "1+1=2") — does that reinforce the idea, or does a reader notice the rerun before they've even reached the diagram?
  • Card 3's mocked Overrides line uses a made-up example (TOK-1, @reza) to show the tier + approver shape — is a fabricated example the right call here, or should it show a real, currently-active override instead?
  • Variant B's diagrams are the same visual weight as the full-map diagram's nodes — does that read as "helpful preview" or as "we already showed you this, twice"?
  • Variant C bets that dx-design deserves top billing over the catalog and DESIGN.md — does that match how people actually reach for this tool, or does it undersell the catalog as an afterthought?
  • None of the three variants use an icon (only mono skill names and diagram notation) — is a wordless icon worth testing as a fourth option, or does this product not have three things distinct enough to iconify without going generic?
