TFX design harness · v0.6.0

The harness now works by intent, and the rulebook knows who it's for.

Seven pieces of work landed on main. In plain terms: you used to pick a tool by its name and hope it fit. Now you say what you want and the right skill answers. And the design rules can finally be scoped to one product or audience instead of applying to everything at once.

7 plans merged 53 controls +5 rules today now live for teams

What changed — before & after

Five everyday things about using the harness, each in one line of before and after.

Finding the right tool
Before

Five skills named after their internals: design, standards, content, onboard, feedback. You had to know which one fit before you could start, and it was easy to pick wrong.

After

You say what you want. "Build a page", "review this", "polish the spacing", "fix the wording" each reach the right skill on their own. If you're unsure, start points you.

The rulebook
Before

48 design rules, and every rule applied to every product and every screen. There was no way to say "this one's only for CaseSync" or "this is for student-facing pages".

After

The same rules — 53 now — taggable by product (Teacher Workspace, CaseSync, Glow) and by audience (teachers, students by age, parents). An untagged rule still means "everyone", so nothing was lost.

What makes each product feel like itself
Before

A product's own colours, tone, and motion were scattered across the catalog and a skill. Hard to find, easy to let drift.

After

Each product gets one DESIGN.md file for its colours, tone, and motion, read automatically whenever you design for it.

Checking the work
Before

Eight-plus separate check scripts, each run by hand. Easy to forget one, and nothing ran on its own.

After

One command, detect, runs the safe set. An optional reminder can nudge you the moment you edit a UI file: quietly, and only when something is actually off.

Getting set up
Before

A guided tour that explained how the harness works.

After

setup installs the tools you actually need: screenshot capture, issue filing, the check scripts. It can also seed a product's DESIGN.md. The tour is still there.

What to do next

In order of leverage. The first one is the point of all the rest.

1
Strategic · biggest payoff

Run it on one real page

All of this is scaffolding until a real product uses it. Nothing has gone through the loop on an actual Teacher Workspace screen yet. Pick one small page and run it end to end. That is the only way to learn whether the upgrade holds up in practice, and it turns "we built a harness" into "we designed a page with it".

2
