## 13 August 2026 — documentation matches the current harness

The website and adopter guide now describe the shipped `dx-design` routing, the
six-phase loop, and the tools that exist today. The guide records each product's
implemented interface stack in `DESIGN.md`; the catalog and check assumptions that
still need generalising are tracked separately.

The standard now speaks across product teams instead of presenting one product
principle as universal. The Settled and Proposed display axis has also been removed
from pages and standards. Older entries below preserve the decisions as they were
recorded at the time.

## 22 July 2026 — content ratified: voice & tone, standards, and guidelines settled

The design lead approved the outstanding content proposals, so they are now the
standard. Record: `docs/catalog-changes/cnt-14-voice-tone.md`.

- **Voice & tone is settled.** The [Voice & tone](/standards/voice-tone)
  guideline adopts Kind Utility and the three voice attributes — Clear,
  Thoughtful, Approachable — with the "we are / we are not" and tone-by-context
  tables. The parallel `voice-tone-proposed` page is retired.
- **CNT-14 reads from the settled page.** The standard's `verify` now points at
  `voice-tone.mdx`, so the enforced voice matches the published one.
- **CNT-5, CNT-6, and CNT-7 are settled.** Device-agnostic action verbs,
  low-informational-value words, and lead-with-purpose lose their Proposed stamp
  and are now binding.
- **The content guidelines are settled.** [Writing](/standards/writing),
  [Grammar & mechanics](/standards/grammar-mechanics), and [Components & text
  patterns](/standards/text-patterns) move from Proposed to settled.
- **The copy skill applies the content standards.** CNT-8 through CNT-13 are
  wired into the copy skill's copy-improvement pass (now `dx-design-copy`),
  and its inline voice table matches the settled guideline.

## 16 July 2026 — the standard stays TFX

The division-level programme (PRs #25 and #30–#34) was cancelled and this repo
stays the TFX (Teacher & School) design standard. Wondo Jeong (design lead)
decided; the record is `docs/decisions/dxd-harvest.md`. Work from that
programme that strengthens the TFX harness was kept and re-expressed here:

- **Checker and validator hardening.** Target expansion prunes vendor
  directories, ignore rules can no longer silence L0 standards, content lints
  see through list markers, the design hook fires on `.ts`/`.js` edits, and
  the eval runner validates commands by parsed path instead of a prefix list.
- **A `status: proposed` pipeline.** A standard can now ship as a visible
  proposal: the schema, validator, build guard, and public projection carry
  the field, and catalog pages render a Proposed badge. CNT-5, CNT-6 and
  CNT-7 carry the stamp, pending the design lead's decision.
- **A rendered contract in CI.** A Playwright suite checks that each route
  renders one main landmark, no horizontal overflow at 320px and 360px, 44px
  mobile targets, and hydration-stable reduced motion. It runs beside the
  Python gate: validator self-test, catalog validation, token audit, and
  static accessibility checks.
- **Site fixes.** The site must pass its own standard: muted foreground
  darkened to meet A11Y-1, the last off-scale type snapped on-scale, uppercase
  chrome removed per TYP-4, and 44px mobile hit boxes on the topbar and
  catalog browser.

The catalog count did not change in the harvest, apart from the two motion
standards proposed below.

## 12 July 2026 — motion tokens; MOT-2 and MOT-3 proposed

The site's own motion layer drifted with no standard to catch it: two surfaces,
two easings, three durations, none declared anywhere. Record:
`docs/catalog-changes/mot-2-mot-3-motion-tokens.md`.

- A declared motion token set: `--motion-fast/base/slow/story` and
  `--ease-out`/`--ease-in-out` in the site's CSS, mirrored for motion/react
  by `lib/motion.ts` and held in sync by a test.
- **MOT-2** (L2): motion values come from the declared token set — durations
  and easings are never hardcoded in component code.
- **MOT-3** (L2): motion may emphasise meaning but never carry it alone — an
  animated surface communicates the same information with animations off.
- Catalog 68 → 70. Both standards are `status: proposed` and wear the Proposed
  badge until the design lead decides.

## 10 July 2026 — content standards and line length

Eight standards extend the catalog's content coverage (merged as PR #29), with
backing detail docs and a wider `content-lint`:

- **CNT-8 through CNT-14.** Plain action verbs over nominalisations; one idea
  per sentence; one term per thing within a product; established UI terms
  teachers already know; sentence case; Singapore English spelling; and copy
  that embodies the TFX voice (Clear, Thoughtful, Approachable).
- **TYP-6.** Running body text is held to a comfortable measure, roughly
  45–75 characters per line.
- A proposed voice and tone page (now [settled](/standards/voice-tone)),
  carried by CNT-14, alongside updates to the writing and grammar guidelines.

## 8 July 2026 — tfx 0.7.0: enforcement as data

- **Catalog 53 → 57 standards.** CMP-4 (empty states signal "no content
  exists"), CMP-8 (non-destructive exits, draft safety), CMP-9 (render-time
  sanitisation of cross-user content), and CNT-4 (content modelling
  real-world artifacts is faithful or labelled illustrative).
- **Enforcement as data.** New `enforced`/`script` fields in the catalog
  schema plus a coverage check, so how a standard is checked lives in the
  catalog itself.
- **Async-state evidence rule.** When loading, success, and error states are
  in scope, the verify evidence must capture all of them — adopted as a
  harness rule rather than a new catalog category.
- **The grill.** The plan gate grew a red-team interview stage: the loop
  questions its own plan, one decision at a time, before asking for approval.

## 1 July 2026 — Glow pilot: catalog maintenance

The whole-app Glow conformance audit surfaced four amendments, approved by the
design lead. No new standard ids and no tier changes. Record:
`docs/catalog-changes/glow-pilot-col1-typ1-tok3.md`.

- **COL-1 re-tagged hybrid.** The standard was tagged deterministic while its
  detail already described a judgment half — a tokenised neutral sitting
  where the brand primary belongs passes the mechanical check.
- **Glow primary settled at `#F76B15`,** with a foreground-pairing rule: a
  light-hue primary takes the scale's dark contrast step, not white, which
  fails AA on this orange.
- **TYP-1 wordmark registry.** A per-product registered-wordmark exception
  (Glow → Inria Sans), scoped to the logo lockup only.
- **TOK-3 coverage caveat.** The token audit does not resolve named Tailwind
  utilities to px, so off-scale named classes stay evaluator-judged for now.
