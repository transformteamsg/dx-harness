Design in code with confidence.

A design harness gives your coding agent a shared design language, the right skills
for each task, and a review before the work comes back to you.

## What the harness gives your agent

Four parts keep every design request grounded in the same language and standards.

**Start with a plain-language request.** `dx-design` understands what you want and
brings in the right skills. Call it directly, or let it step in when a request needs
design work.

**Shared design guidance agents can use.** The control catalog turns good interface
design into clear context that every skill can read and check.

**Your product's design language.** Keep the decisions that make your product
distinct in its repository. Every agent works from the same `DESIGN.md` context as
the product grows.

**A review grounded in both.** A separate reviewer checks the work against the
control catalog and `DESIGN.md`, then returns specific findings before the work
ships.

## From a request to a reviewed result

Speak your intent, collaborate on the same standard, and get better design outcomes.

1. **Your prompt**, in Claude Code: “Make this lesson planner easier to scan and keep
   it consistent with our product.”
2. **The harness at work**, running automatically: `dx-design` brings in the skills
   this request needs. Each one reads the control catalog and your `DESIGN.md`.
3. **A reviewed result**, back in your product: Execute makes the approved change. A
   separate review checks the result before it comes back to you.

## Meet your new collaborators

Six roles cover the path from an open request to a reviewed interface. DX Design
calls the right ones for you.

| Role | What it does | Command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orchestrator | Understands the request and brings in the skills it needs. | `/dx-harness:dx-design` |
| Copy | Makes interface language clear, useful, and consistent. | `/dx-harness:dx-design-copy` |
| Pattern | Sets the structure, hierarchy, and density of the page. | `/dx-harness:dx-design-pattern` |
| Polish | Refines spacing, type, colour, and the small details. | `/dx-harness:dx-design-polish` |
| Execute | Turns the approved direction into working product code. | `/dx-harness:dx-design-execute` |
| Review | Checks the result against the control catalog and `DESIGN.md`. | Runs automatically after execute |

See [all the skills](/harness/skills).

## A shared language for you and your agent

DX Harness bridges human judgment and agent execution. It gives both of you the
same design language and the checks to build safely on each other's work.
