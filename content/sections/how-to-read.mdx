## The ladder

| Layer | Answers | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[The catalog](/standards/catalog)** | Must | Required; verified by checks. L0 blocks, L1 needs a documented waiver |
| **[The guidance pages](/standards/writing)** | Should | Judgement applies; deviation needs a reason. Build from the specimens by default |
| **[Products](/products)** | Where | One character, calibrated per product |
| **[Harness](/harness)** | How, fast | Use the skills and tools; improve them |

> **The one litmus test:** if you can't check it, it's guidance and lives in a guidance page's prose, not in the catalog. The catalog is the only layer the harness can enforce automatically.

## Mission

Make the quality bar independent of staffing. A builder's intent should survive intact all the way to shipped UI that meets our bar in aesthetics and UX, and get there faster. The harness carries the repetitive 80%; your judgement goes to the hard, novel 20%.
