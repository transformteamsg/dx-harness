Our voice is guided by **Kind Utility**: useful at the core, human at the surface. Teachers use our products to complete tasks quickly, often while managing many responsibilities and working across different systems. We speak **clearly, thoughtfully, and approachably** so teachers can understand what to do, feel supported, and complete tasks with confidence.

Teachers use our products to complete tasks quickly, often while managing many other responsibilities. Some may also have low tech confidence, so our voice should make the product feel clear, thoughtful, and approachable.

Our **voice** is **constant**. It is how we sound across products and product moments. Our **tone adapts** to context and user needs, such as onboarding, success states, errors, and sensitive actions.

Voice helps us sound consistent. Tone helps us respond with empathy. [CNT-14](/standards/catalog/cnt-14) is a normative check on voice and tone; the dx-design-copy skill applies it too, so UI text arrives on brand.

## Voice attributes

Our voice is:

1. **Clear** — We are plainspoken and precise, so teachers can easily understand what something means and what to do next.
2. **Thoughtful** — We respect teachers' time, attention, and context. We give the right amount of guidance without adding noise.
3. **Approachable** — We help teachers feel at ease and supported, especially in unfamiliar or stressful flows.

All of this means that when we write UI text:

{/* dx-sync:voice-attributes */}
| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Clear but not cold | Robotic or detached |
| Simple but not simplistic | Vague, generic or too minimal to be useful |
| Thoughtful but not wordy | Repetitive or too detailed |
| Helpful but not overbearing | Patronising or overly prescriptive |
| Approachable but not careless | Corporate speak or overly casual |
| Reassuring but not sappy | Alarmist or dramatic |
{/* /dx-sync:voice-attributes */}

## Tone by context

{/* dx-sync:tone-context */}
| Context | Tone | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Success | Affirming, brief | Acknowledge, don't gush |
| Error | Calm, helpful | What happened → what it means → what to do next |
| Onboarding | Encouraging | Lower the stakes; show the quick win |
| Destructive action | Sober, precise | Plain consequences, no drama ([CMP-2](/standards/catalog/cmp-2)) |
| Empty state | Inviting | Lead with the next action |
| Permission / data request | Transparent, respectful | Say what's collected, why, and how it's used — before asking |
{/* /dx-sync:tone-context */}
