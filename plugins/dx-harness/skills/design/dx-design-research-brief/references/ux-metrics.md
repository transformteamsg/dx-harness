# UX metrics

Guidance for component 7 of an evaluative or hybrid study. A generative study does not
read this file.

### 7. UX metrics

Where the study is quantitative, list the metrics you will track:

- Time on task
- Task success (pass or fail, or a three-point scale)
- Number of errors
- SEQ (Single Ease Question)
- UMUX or SUS
- Other, such as SMEQ or confidence
