Tidy a document so it reads well.

## What to do

Read the document and improve it. Use your judgement about what needs changing: some documents need a light touch and others need more work, and you will know the difference when you see it.

Fix the obvious problems first. Then look at the structure and see whether it holds together.

## Things to watch

- Long sections should usually be broken up, unless they read fine as they are.
- Headings should be helpful.
- Remove anything that is not needed.
- Keep the author's voice where it is worth keeping.

## When to stop

Stop when the document is in good shape. Do not over-edit.
