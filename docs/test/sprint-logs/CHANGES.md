# House style changes, side by side

Every change the house style made to the three Squad Charlie sprint logs, one changed
paragraph per row. The edits are wrapped in ⟦brackets⟧ on both sides, so they do not get
confused with the logs' own **bold**.

Read this instead of diffing the files. These logs hard-wrap at about 90 characters, so a
one-word fix reflows the whole paragraph, and a line diff then reports the paragraph rather
than the edit.

The files sit beside this one: `before-<workstream>.md` and `after-<workstream>.md`. Long
URLs are shortened here for column width; the files carry them in full.

**Nothing in `transformteamsg/tfx-brain` was modified.** These are evaluation copies.

## How to review this

Work down the table and ask one question per row: **does the after column make that passage
harder to act on?** That is the failure this benchmark exists to catch, and no word count
will show it.

Three groups carry judgement rather than mechanics, so they are worth a second look:

- **The "One action, one step" rows** turn a paragraph into numbered steps. Each is longer
  than what it replaced. Judge whether making the order explicit earns the extra words.
- **The "Restates another section" row** is the only place a sentence was dropped for
  repeating another section. Check that the risk still reads as a risk without it.
- **The "Self-certifying word" rows** cut `actually`, `real` or `genuine`. In two of them
  the word arguably marked a contrast between production and mocked data, which is the
  documented exception to that rule. Say if you think either should go back.
