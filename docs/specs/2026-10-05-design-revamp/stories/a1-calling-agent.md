# A1: calling agent

Part of the [design revamp stories](README.md). Confirmed through [#419](https://github.com/transformteamsg/dx-harness/issues/419).

- **Background:** Another skill, a subagent, or an unattended run.
- **What they want from the harness:** One contract: inputs, outputs, and no questions to a human.

## A1-1: Dispatch a review

As a calling agent, I want to dispatch a review of one surface and get findings back, with no questions to a human.
