# P3: design lead

Part of the [design revamp stories](README.md). Confirmed through [#417](https://github.com/transformteamsg/dx-harness/issues/417).

- **Background:** Owns the catalogue, each product's `DESIGN.md`, and L1 waivers.
- **What they want from the harness:** Consistent quality across products, and a controlled way for rules to change.

## P3-1: Control waivers and standing overrides

As a design lead, I want to approve a waiver or promote a repeated waiver to a standing override, so that exceptions stay controlled.
