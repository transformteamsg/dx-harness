Book a community hall

Use this service to book a hall for your group.

Before you start

  • Who can apply: any registered group in Singapore.
  • Time: it takes about ten minutes to finish.
  • Documents you need: your group registration number.
  • Fee: S$25.00 for each booking.
  • You can pay by credit card.

Refund policy: we refund the fee if you cancel one week before the date.

We confirm your booking within 3 working days.
